## Scope and remaining limits

For each fixed q in (0,1) and η in (0,1), the reported theorem supplies compact E in [0,1] of measure greater than 1−η excluding every x+s{qⁿ:n≥1}, for arbitrary x and either sign of nonzero s. E may depend on q. This is a special case of the general similarity conjecture, not a simultaneous all-ratios statement.

The finite-pattern precursor is commonly attributed to Steinhaus's 1920 paper. Its original full text was not inspected, so conceptual origins and the background timeline event use **unknown** precision rather than assigning a priority date. The 1974 printed formulation is directly checked; earlier private priority is not claimed. Original Bourgain full text was inaccessible; the additive result is documented in Kolountzakis's primary account. No manuscript proof or independent acceptance was assessed.
