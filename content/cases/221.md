Sparse connections do not make disorder simple. The Viana–Bray model of 1985 supplied a classical diluted setting, with a finite mean number of interactions per spin.

In their [2000 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/cond-mat/0009418v1), published in 2001, Mézard and Parisi described the fixed-connectivity spin glass through the cavity method. Competing states required distributions of local-field distributions. Their explicit solution worked at one step of replica symmetry breaking.

[Franz and Leone](https://arxiv.org/html/cond-mat/0208280v1) turned selected predictions into rigorous bounds. In 2004, [Panchenko and Talagrand](https://arxiv.org/html/math/0405357v1) extended those bounds to arbitrary finite depth and explicitly conjectured that their infimum was exact.

The next steps branched. Panchenko obtained an exact variational description through invariant spin distributions, then a hierarchical representation in the finite-RSB case for modified models. A general approximation remained missing. Biswas, Chen and Sen later established simpler formulas in replica-symmetric regimes.

The 2026 OpenAI manuscript reports the hierarchical equality for the stated Poisson even-arity class. Its scope joins a physical picture to a precise variational conjecture; independent proof assessment remains separate.
